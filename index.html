⚡ Localization decision spotlight — "Compose your violence"

Source term (EN)

"Compose your violence" — uses a musical metaphor. "Compose" evokes authorship, artistry, the act of writing a score. Deliberately poetic and unusual.

Options considered (CN)

谱写你的暴力 — direct equivalent, preserves the musical metaphor (谱写 = to compose/write a musical score). 书写你的暴力 — more neutral "write." 演绎你的暴力 — "perform/interpret," loses authorship angle.

Final decision

谱写你的暴力 — retained as the primary translation. 谱写 carries the same musical-authorship connotation in Chinese, making this a rare case where the metaphor transfers cleanly. Used as a thematic throughline across the text.

Terminology management

On large-scale projects, consistency doesn't come from memory — it comes from infrastructure. Below is an extract from the CN–SR–EN terminology database developed across railway, legal, manufacturing, and financial domains. Each entry includes a domain rationale, not just a translation.

ChineseSerbianEnglishDomainRationale
接触网 kontaktna mreža overhead contact line system Railway Refers to the full catenary system — not just the contact wire. "Overhead wire" would be incomplete.
正线 pravilni kolosek main line track Railway SR distinguishes 正线 (pravilni) from 到发线 (prijemno-otpravni). Conflating them risks operational misreading.
停电作业 rad bez napona de-energized work Safety Preferred over "power-off work" — aligns with IEC 60050 electrical safety terminology across all three languages.
压光工艺 kalandriranje calendering process Manufacturing Technical textile process. Not to be confused with "calendering" in printing — different equipment, different outcome.

形式发票 profaktura pro forma invoice Financial SR "profaktura" is the established commercial term. Calque "forma faktura" would be non-standard and confusing.
